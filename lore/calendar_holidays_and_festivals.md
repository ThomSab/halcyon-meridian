# Calendar, Holidays, and Festivals Aboard the Halcyon Meridian

## Calendar

The ship uses the Gregorian calendar and a 24-hour clock. Its clock is set to the time zone of the departure port, and it changes one hour at a time to match the ship's longitude (see `daily_life_and_routines.md`). The ship's official date is the date on the ship's registry, which is used for pay, contracts, and records.

The working week is seven days, with a rota that gives most residents at least one day off per week. Crew are on a rotating schedule, and the Company guarantees at least one full day off in every seven for non-emergency work.

## Ship's Anniversaries

The ship marks several anniversaries:

| Date | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| March | **Launch Day** | Anniversary of the launch from the Ferrol basin in 2065 |
| September | **Departure Day** | Anniversary of the maiden voyage from Lisbon in 2066 |
| November | **Articles Day** | Anniversary of the Articles of Agreement, ratified in 2065 |

Launch Day and Departure Day are full holidays. Articles Day is a half holiday, with a public reading of the Articles in the Spine Theatre. Each anniversary includes a community meal in the market halls, paid for by the Company.

## Holidays

Residents' own religious and national holidays are recognized, subject to the work rota. The Company designates a number of public holidays each year, which are paid at double time for those who work them. The most widely observed holidays include:

- **New Year's Day**, using the ship's date
- **Lunar New Year**, celebrated with lanterns and a parade along the Spine
- **Diwali**, with lights in the terraces and sweets in the market halls
- **Eid al-Fitr** and **Eid al-Adha**, with prayers in the mosques and festive meals
- **Christmas**, with carol services and a shared meal in the Central Hall
- **Vaisakhi**, **Nowruz**, and other seasonal holidays, as requested by residents

The Chaplaincy and the Residents' Council set the list of recognized holidays together (see `religion_and_chaplaincy.md`).

## Crossing the Line

When the ship crosses the equator, the **Crossing the Line** ceremony is held. This is an old maritime tradition, and on the *Halcyon Meridian* it has been adapted to include residents, not only crew. Participants who have never crossed the equator before are initiated, with a short ceremony and a certificate from the Master. The ceremony is held on the Spine. It is popular with children.

## Landfall Festival

After each major port call, the Company holds a **Landfall Festival** on the Spine. It includes music, food from the port, and a market in the Harbour Hall. The festival is paid for by the Company's community programme (see `ports_of_call.md`).

## Calendar of Local Events

Each month includes at least one scheduled event:
- A monthly fire and muster drill (see `emergency_response_and_evacuation.md`)
- A monthly library book sale, when lost or retired items are sold
- A quarterly market price review (see `markets_and_commerce.md`)
- A quarterly Council open session, where residents may question the Council's committees

## Time Changes

Some holidays fall at inconvenient times because of the ship's clock. When a holiday begins at sunset on shore, the Chaplaincy publishes the start time in ship time, using the ship's position. This is most noticeable during Ramadan.

## Weather and Holidays

Holidays are never cancelled for weather. However, large public events are moved indoors or to the Solarium deck in storms. Storm Law may suspend public events, and the Master decides when this is necessary (see `governance_and_law.md`).

## Records

The ship's annual calendar is published on the intranet each December for the following year. The calendar is also printed in the Halcyon Gazette (see `arts_media_and_entertainment.md`).
