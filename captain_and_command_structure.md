# Captain and Command Structure of the Halcyon Meridian

## The Master

The captain is formally titled the **Master**. Under maritime law, the Master has ultimate authority over the safety of the vessel and everyone on it. This authority is established by the International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea (SOLAS) and the Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping (STCW), which require a Master to hold an unlimited command certificate.

The current Master is **Captain Teodora Ansah**, age 58, born in Accra. She has 22 years on container ships and 9 years on large cruise vessels before joining the *Halcyon Meridian* at launch. She is known for:

- Speaking little in meetings and a great deal on the bridge
- Walking the full length of the Spine every morning at 06:00 to observe conditions
- Reading every incident report personally, including minor ones
- Sleeping four to six hours a night in a 90 m² cabin on the bridge deck
- Keeping a paper logbook alongside the electronic one, a habit she calls "a second memory"

Her weakness, by her own account, is distrust of anything that runs without her attention.

## Who Holds Power

The Master does not own the ship and does not run its business. Authority is divided among three bodies:

1. **The Board of Directors** of Meridian Maritime Holdings, the owning company. The Board meets quarterly, mostly ashore. It appoints and dismisses the Master and approves the annual budget.
2. **The Master**, who commands the ship at sea and has emergency powers over all persons aboard.
3. **The Residents' Council**, elected by residents, which ratifies some budget items and can call referenda on food and water prices (see `governance_and_law.md`).

The Master answers to the Board for business decisions and to maritime law for safety decisions. In practice, most conflicts arise when the Board wants cost savings that the Master believes compromise safety.

## Chain of Command

| Position | Responsibility | Typical Staff |
|---|---|---|
| **Master** | Overall command, legal authority | 1 |
| **Staff Captain** (second-in-command) | Deck operations, succession | 1 |
| **Chief Engineer** | Reactors, propulsion, power, damage control | ~6,000 |
| **Chief Operating Officer** (Company) | Commerce, freight, budgets, port agreements | Appointed by Board |
| **Chief Warden** | Security, policing, detentions | ~2,500 |
| **Chief Medical Officer** | Hospital, public health, quarantine | ~1,800 |
| **Hotel Director** | Housing, water, waste, food commissary | ~11,000 |
| **Sail Master** | Wingsail operation and stowage | ~1,200 |

Two separate command centers exist:
- **The Bridge**, in the forward superstructure, handles navigation and ship control.
- **The City Operations Centre** (COC), in the aft tower, handles residential services, power distribution to homes, public health, and emergency response to incidents inside the city.

During a storm or emergency, the Master can direct the COC directly.

## Succession

If the Master is incapacitated, the Staff Captain assumes command. If the Staff Captain is also unavailable, the Articles of Agreement name the next officer in line, in order. The Board must confirm any change within 72 hours.

## Legal Powers of the Master

Under the Company Articles and maritime law, the Master may:
- Order the ship to change course, speed, or sail configuration
- Detain any person for up to 48 hours pending a hearing
- Declare a state of Storm Law, which temporarily restricts movement between decks
- Witness and certify births, deaths, and marriages for the ship's registry
- Refuse to sail if the vessel is unsafe, regardless of commercial pressure

The Master cannot override the Board on contracts or pricing, but can refuse to carry out an order that endangers the ship.

## Daily Schedule (Typical)

- **05:30** Weather briefing with the Navigation Officer and Sail Master
- **06:00** Spine walk, observing conditions and speaking with residents
- **07:00** Operations meeting with department heads
- **09:00** Review of incident reports and the paper logbook
- **13:00** Meeting with the Residents' Council liaison, as needed
- **18:00** Evening bridge round and watch handover
- **22:00** Written reports for the Board, transmitted via satellite
